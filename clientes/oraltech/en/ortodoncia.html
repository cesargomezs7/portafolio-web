Orthodontics with conventional braces,
sapphire or clear aligners

Three ways to straighten your teeth and correct your bite. Here is how they differ, so you can choose with confidence, in Cúcuta and in Medellín.

Why it matters

It straightens your teeth and your bite too

It restores the balance of the mouth and the face, and with it facial esthetics.

What changes from one option to the next is the appliance: conventional brackets, sapphire crystal brackets, or clear removable aligners.

The three options

Conventional, sapphire or clear aligners

Conventional braces

They study, prevent and correct developmental problems, the shape of the dental arches and the position of the jaws.

What they aim for
  • Restore the structural balance of the mouth and the face
  • Restore functional balance
  • Improve facial esthetics as well
What they correct: developmental problems, the shape of the dental arches and the position of the jaws.

Sapphire braces

Brackets made of sapphire crystal, one of the strongest materials there is.

Benefits
  • Just as effective as conventional brackets
  • Sapphire crystal, one of the strongest materials there is
  • Clear
  • They do not stain or turn yellow
What sets them apart: they are clear and do not change color the way other types of braces can.

Clear aligners

Clear, removable aligners that transform your smile without interfering with your lifestyle.

Benefits
  • Clear, removable aligners
  • No visible brackets
  • No restrictions on talking, eating or smiling
  • Esthetic and functional results
Ideal for: anyone looking for esthetic and functional results without visible brackets.
