Dental cleaning to remove plaque and tartar

A dental cleaning —or prophylaxis— is a professional procedure that removes plaque and tartar from your teeth and gums. We do it at both of our locations: Quinta Oriental in Cúcuta and El Poblado in Medellín.

What it is

A professional procedure

A dental cleaning, or dental prophylaxis, is a professional procedure performed by a dentist or dental hygienist to remove plaque —a soft, sticky film— and tartar —hardened, mineralized plaque— from the teeth and gums.

At OralTech we do it at both locations, Cúcuta and Medellín. If you have questions about your case, message us on WhatsApp before booking and we'll walk you through it.

What we remove

Plaque and tartar are not the same

Dental plaque

A soft, sticky film that builds up on the teeth and along the gumline. It's the one you keep under control with day-to-day brushing.

Tartar

Plaque that has hardened and mineralized. Once it reaches that point a toothbrush won't take it off: it has to be removed in the office.

Teeth and gums

A cleaning doesn't stop at the visible surface of the tooth: plaque and tartar are also removed from along the gumline.

Often confused

A cleaning is not whitening

A dental cleaning removes plaque and tartar. Teeth whitening is a different treatment: a cosmetic dentistry procedure that lightens the teeth by removing stains.

They're two different things and they're booked separately. If what you want is to lighten the shade of your teeth, take a look at teeth whitening.
