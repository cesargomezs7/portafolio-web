Micro smile design without grinding down your teeth

A minimally invasive cosmetic technique that improves the shape, size, color or alignment of your teeth, with natural, harmonious results. In Cúcuta and Medellín.

Why it matters

You don't always need a full makeover

Micro smile design is a minimally invasive cosmetic technique that refines the small details of your smile: the shape, size, color or alignment of your teeth. Using materials such as composite resin or ceromer, we get natural, harmonious results without grinding down the tooth, preserving its structure.

If what you want is a bigger change, the right technique is smile design, which can also be done in porcelain, not just ceromer and composite resin. Message us on WhatsApp, tell us what you'd like to change and we'll tell you which of the two makes sense for you.
