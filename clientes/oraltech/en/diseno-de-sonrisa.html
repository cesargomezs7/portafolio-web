Smile design in composite resin, ceromer or porcelain

Three ways to redesign your smile, each with its own material and its own ideal case. We walk you through the differences so you can choose with confidence. We are OralTech, and we are in Cúcuta and Medellín.

Why it matters

The material changes the result

Smile design improves the shape, size and color of your teeth. What changes from one option to the next is the material it is made with: composite resin, ceromer or porcelain.

Each material has its strength — cost, balance or maximum esthetics — and its ideal case. Message us on WhatsApp, tell us what you want to change and we will tell you which of the three makes sense for you.

The three options

Composite resin, ceromer or porcelain

Smile design in composite resin

A versatile, accessible material that allows for fast, minimally invasive esthetic changes.

Benefits
  • The most affordable procedure
  • Conservative with your natural tooth
  • Easy to maintain
Best for subtle or temporary changes.

Smile design in ceromer

A hybrid material that combines the esthetics of porcelain with the flexibility of composite resin.

Benefits
  • Natural appearance
  • High strength
  • Minimal wear on the tooth
  • Excellent fit
Best for anyone looking for a balance between durability and esthetics.

Smile design in porcelain

The most durable and most esthetic material. It looks almost exactly like a natural tooth.

Benefits
  • Maximum strength
  • High esthetics
  • Resists staining
  • A whiter, more harmonious smile
Best for anyone looking for premium, long-term results.

Questions about smile design

What patients ask us most

It depends on what you are looking for. Composite resin is the most affordable option and the most conservative with your natural tooth, ideal for subtle or temporary changes. Ceromer strikes a balance between durability and esthetics. Porcelain is the strongest and most esthetic of the three, made for premium, long-term results. Message us on WhatsApp and we will guide you based on your case.

It depends on the material. Composite resin is conservative with the tooth and easy to maintain, and ceromer causes only minimal wear on the tooth. At your consultation we explain what each option means for your specific case.

Porcelain does not stain easily. It is the most durable and most esthetic of the three materials, and it looks almost exactly like a natural tooth.

The price depends on the option you choose and on how many teeth are treated, so we would rather not publish a generic figure that later does not match your case. Message us on WhatsApp, tell us what you want to change and we will give you concrete information.
