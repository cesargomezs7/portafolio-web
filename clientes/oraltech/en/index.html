Micro smile design is a minimally invasive cosmetic technique that improves small details of your smile: the shape, size, color or alignment of your teeth, with no tooth reduction and preserving the natural structure. Smile design is broader work on the smile as a whole, and it can be done in composite resin, ceromer or porcelain.

We work with three materials. Composite resin is the most affordable option, conservative with the tooth and easy to maintain, ideal for subtle or temporary changes. Ceromer combines the look of porcelain with the flexibility of resin: a natural appearance, high strength and minimal wear on the tooth. Porcelain offers the greatest durability and the best esthetics, it does not stain easily and it is the right choice for long-term results.

Traditional braces, sapphire braces and clear aligners. Sapphire braces are just as effective as traditional ones, they are made of sapphire crystal and they are clear: they do not stain or turn yellow. Clear aligners are transparent and removable, with no visible brackets and no restrictions on talking, eating or smiling.

A dental implant is a screw-shaped medical device placed in the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. It works as the anchor for an artificial tooth, the crown. If you are missing a tooth, message us and we will evaluate your case.

A dental cleaning, also called prophylaxis, is a professional procedure performed by a dentist or dental hygienist to remove plaque, a soft sticky film, and tartar, which is hardened mineralized plaque, from the teeth and gums.

Teeth whitening is a cosmetic dentistry procedure that lightens the teeth by removing stains. It is painless, it leaves a better looking smile and it preserves your oral health.
